Ergonomics

Look straight ahead, not down

Conventional loupes converge your eyes toward a single close point, and to see the field you tip your head down and hold it there. PENTAX Loupes are built around a different idea: your eyes stay level, and the optics do the bending.

Conventional loupes. The head tips forward so the eyes can converge on one close point, and stays there for the whole procedure.

PENTAX Loupes. The head stays level and the eyes look straight ahead. Prisms inside the barrels bend the optical path down to the field.

Deflection prisms

Prisms inside each barrel redirect the optical path, much like working with a microscope. You look straight ahead at your work instead of angling your eyes down, which helps you hold a natural, upright posture through long procedures.

Parallel viewing

Conventional loupes converge your eyes toward a single close point. PENTAX Loupes are built for parallel viewing instead, designed to reduce eye convergence and help support an upright working posture.

Questions

What clinicians ask us

Is the magnification adjustable?

No. Magnification is fixed when the loupes are built, which gives a consistent, predictable image. Pupillary distance and working distance are adjustable by you.

Can I adjust the fit later?

Yes. Pupillary distance and working distance can be reset as your eyesight or working posture changes, without returning the loupes to the factory.

Can I wear them with my own glasses?

Yes. Many clinicians do not need separate glasses at all. If you do, a different nose pad makes room for your own glasses underneath.

Which magnification suits my work?

As a guide, dentists commonly use 2.5× to 3.5× (aesthetic dentists often 5.0×), surgeons 3.5× to 5.0×, veterinary surgeons 2.5× to 3.5×. Your specialist helps you choose at your fitting.

Can I try them before I decide?

Yes. We arrange a demonstration and fitting with you in the UK, so you see the view for yourself before anything is ordered.

What do they cost?

It depends on the magnification and on options such as LED lighting. Your specialist prepares a personalised quotation for your configuration.

What warranty do they come with?

A lifetime warranty. The terms come with your quotation.

Are LED lights available?

Yes, wired or wireless. If you already own a light you like, brackets are available for other LED brands.
