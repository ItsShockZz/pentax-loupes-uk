Cookies policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version

This website does not set cookies. That is why you do not see a cookie banner: there is nothing to ask your consent for.

The detail

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, social media embeds or third-party fonts. The typeface, images and videos on this site are all served from our own domain, so visiting this site does not send your IP address to font or media networks. The site does not read or write cookies, localStorage or any other browser storage.

When you submit a form, the information you typed is sent to us once, when you press the button. Nothing is collected in the background while you browse.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operations. These are not cookies and are covered in our privacy policy.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we will update this page first, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent with a clear banner before anything is set.

Managing cookies generally

Every modern browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its settings. Instructions for your browser are on your browser maker's help pages.
