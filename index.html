Technology

Why PENTAX

Four things the optical design does differently from conventional loupes.

01

Balance point

A lightweight frame with balanced weight distribution, so the loupes sit evenly and stay comfortable through long procedures rather than dragging on your nose.

02

A wide field of view

Japanese optics give a sharp, distortion-free image across a wide field, with the depth perception fine work needs. At 2.5× the field of view is 141 mm.

03

Parallel viewing

Conventional loupes converge your eyes toward a single close point. PENTAX Loupes are built for parallel viewing instead, designed to reduce eye convergence and help support an upright working posture.

04

Flip up, flip down

Flip the barrels out of the way to switch instantly between magnified and normal vision, without taking the frame off.

Prescription & lens options

Made for your eyes

Magnification is fixed and does not correct your everyday vision. There are three ways to pair PENTAX Loupes with your prescription.

Adjustable frame, no glasses

Many clinicians do not need separate glasses at all. The frame adjusts directly to your vision.

Your own glasses underneath

A different nose pad creates room to wear your existing glasses under the loupes.

Custom prescription insert

A clip-in insert carries your correction inside the PENTAX frame itself.

Ownership, done properly

Why the passport matters

A precision instrument deserves better aftercare than a receipt in a drawer. The passport is how a PENTAX pair stays a PENTAX pair, for you and for whoever wears it next.

01

Sell on, the proper way

If you ever want to sell your loupes on, the passport is how it's done: the record is transferred to the new owner, who gets their own fitting and the same UK support, and the pair stays a genuine, traceable PENTAX. Sold without it, a pair is just a second-hand pair.

02

The manufacturer's warranty, protected

A passported pair is a genuine PENTAX pair supplied through the UK team and covered by the manufacturer's warranty. No grey imports, no guesswork about where it came from. That protects you, and it protects the name on the frame.

03

Reassurance if something's off

A slipping nose pad, a headlight question, a fitting that needs a tweak: one tap on your passport reaches a real person, with your setup already in front of them.

04

Your numbers, kept

Magnification, working distance and pupillary distance are recorded at your fitting, so re-fits, adjustments and accessories start from your numbers rather than from scratch.

05

Care guidance, always at hand

Cleaning, handling and storage guidance for your pair lives on your page, so the optics stay sharp for years and the adjustments made at your fitting stay put.

06

Nothing to install

No app, no account, no password. A card in the case and a link that is yours alone, for as long as you wear them.

Loupes are small magnifying optical instruments, typically worn as eyeglasses. They give a close-up view of fine detail that's difficult to see with the naked eye, and are widely used for precision work.

Dentists, prosthodontists and dental hygienists, surgeons, hair transplant specialists, permanent makeup artists, veterinary surgeons, and other professionals whose work requires enhanced close-up vision.

Through-the-lens (TTL) loupes are fixed and custom-fitted, with pupillary distance set at the factory. Front-lens-mounted loupes clip in front of separate eyeglasses. PENTAX Loupes are flip-up loupes: pupillary distance and working distance can be adjusted by the user, and the loupes can flip out of the way for regular vision.
