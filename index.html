02 — VIEWING ANGLE

Look forward. Work naturally.

Conventional loupes converge your eyes toward a single close point. PENTAX Loupes are designed for parallel viewing instead — intended to reduce eye convergence and help support a more natural working posture, reducing unnecessary neck flexion over long procedures.

03 — FRAME

Designed to disappear while you work.

A lightweight frame with balanced weight distribution, so the loupes don't feel heavy even after long procedures. The nosepad and frame are shaped for comfortable, ergonomic wear.

04 — FIT

A fit that's personal to you.

The nose bridge and nose pad are adjustable to your face. Some clinicians experience discomfort with standard nose pads — solutions exist, and our specialists can talk you through them.

Technology

Why PENTAX

Four principles behind the optical design — not marketing claims, but the mechanics of how PENTAX Loupes differ from conventional loupes.

01

Unique deflection prisms

PENTAX Loupes use deflection prisms to redirect the optical path, letting you look straight ahead — much like working with a microscope — rather than angling your eyes down toward the working area.

02

A wide field of view

Superior optical quality gives a clear, sharp, distortion-free image with a wide field of view and strong depth perception, built on Japanese optics.

03

Parallel viewing

Conventional loupes converge your eyes toward a single close point. PENTAX Loupes are designed for parallel viewing instead, intended to reduce eye convergence and help support a more natural, upright working posture.

04

Easy transition between views

Flip the loupes out of the way to move instantly between magnified and normal-size viewing, without removing the frame.

Prescription & lens options

Made for your eyes. Including your prescription.

Magnification itself is fixed and doesn't correct your everyday vision — but there are three straightforward ways to use PENTAX Loupes alongside your prescription.

Adjustable frame, no glasses needed

Most clinicians don't need separate glasses at all — the frame adjusts directly to your vision.

Wear your own glasses underneath

Change the nose pad to create enough room to comfortably wear your existing prescription glasses under the loupes.

Custom prescription insert

A clip-in insert fits the PENTAX frame directly and provides your correction, without wearing separate glasses.
